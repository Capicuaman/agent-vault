## Channel Rules

### Instagram / TikTok
- **Format:** 1080×1350 (4:5 feed), 1080×1920 (Stories/Reels).
- **Tone:** Energetic, visual, myth-busting. Lead with the hook in the first 1.5 seconds.
- **Hashtag strategy:** Mix of branded (#bilan, #TuSaludEnNumeros), niche (#saludmetabolica, #biohackingmx), and broad (#salud, #bienestar).
- **CTA:** "Link in bio" — always verify the bio link is current.

### LinkedIn
- **Format:** Text posts with line breaks, PDF carousels for reports, articles for thought leadership.
- **Tone:** Professional, data-backed, industry-relevant.
- **Focus:** Corporate wellness, metabolic health trends in LatAm, employer ROI.

### Email / Newsletter
- **Subject line rules:** ≤50 chars, front-load benefit, use numbers when possible.
- **Preview text:** Treat as a second headline; never leave as default.
- **Body:** Single-column, mobile-first, max 600px width. CTA button in bilan Blue (`#0066CC`).
- **Bilingual:** Send Spanish by default; offer English toggle or segment by preference.

## Content Calendar Conventions

- **Monday:** Educational (blog drop, carousel)
- **Wednesday:** Social proof (testimonial, case study)
- **Friday:** Engagement (poll, myth-buster, AMA teaser)
- **Monthly:** Newsletter (first Tuesday of the month)
- **Quarterly:** Campaign push (new product feature, corporate wellness push)

### Ad Creative Patterns
- Problem-solution-reveal: "Tired after lunch? [Blood sugar spike] → [Metabolic Panel] → [See the data]"
- Social proof: "[X] empleados de [Company] ya conocen sus números"
- Urgency: "Cupón de lanzamiento: 30% hasta [Date]"

## Compliance Notes

- **Health claims:** Do not promise cure, treatment, or diagnosis. Use "screening," "insights," "understand your markers."
- **Testimonials:** Must include "Results may vary." Attribute to first name + city.
- **Promotions:** Clearly state expiration dates and terms. Never use "limited time" without an actual deadline.
