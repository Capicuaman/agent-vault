# Case Study — Real Estate Broker Interview (Mazatlán)

**Context:** Three siblings evaluating a beachfront property in Mazatlán belonging to their ill mother. Three paths under consideration: sell now (preserve ISR exemption), renovate and sell, renovate and rent. The counterparty (broker) has strong structural bias toward "sell now" (fastest commission).

## Files

- **Original interrogation-style script** (checklist of ~75 direct questions):
  `01_PROJECTS/CASA_MAZATLAN/03_pricing/reunion-corredor-preguntas.md`
- **Elicitation-style script** (companion, conversational):
  `01_PROJECTS/CASA_MAZATLAN/03_pricing/reunion-corredor-elicitacion.md`

## Design decisions worth reusing

1. **Kept both versions.** Interrogation checklist = internal what-you-need-to-know. Elicitation script = how-you-actually-talk. Don't replace one with the other.

2. **Block-by-block routing:**
   - **Screening block** — semi-direct with softeners (you want the counterparty to know they're being evaluated; competitive filter)
   - **Market / price / opinion blocks** — elicitation-first (max structural bias, max filtering)
   - **Contracts / terms block** — fully direct (legal clarity trumps rapport)

3. **Opened with competition + human context.**
   > "Estamos hablando con 2 o 3 corredores porque es la casa de nuestra mamá."
   This single sentence deactivates commercial autopilot and activates human-advisor mode.

4. **Two-hermano rule** — one asks, one only writes; rotate every 15 min. Debrief only after.

5. **Cheat sheet of 12 Spanish phrases** at the end, memorizable, deployable under pressure.

6. **Post-meeting evaluation grid** rewards behaviors that indicate real expertise:
   - Corrected false statements with data
   - Filled silences with cifras
   - Recognized own structural bias unprompted
   - Delivered follow-up in 48h without chasing

## What generalizes to other domains

The 6-block structure works for any high-stakes service-provider interview:

| Block | Direct or Elicit |
|---|---|
| 1. Screening credentials | Semi-direct |
| 2. Market / pricing intel | Elicit-first |
| 3. Alternatives / options | Elicit-first |
| 4. Investment / preparation | Elicit-first |
| 5. Honest recommendation | Elicit-only |
| 6. Terms / contract | Direct-only |
