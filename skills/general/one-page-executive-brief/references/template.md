# One-Page Executive Brief Template

Use this as a skeleton, not a rigid form.

```markdown
# [project / decision title]

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  
**Source:** `path/to/longer/source.md`  
**Prepared for:** [leadership / founder / board / team]

## Decision summary
- [core recommendation in one sentence]
- [secondary decision or sequencing logic]

## Why this matters
- [business / strategic reason]
- [timing reason]
- [risk of inaction]

## Recommendation
### 1) [first move]
- [why]

### 2) [second move]
- [why]

## Key risks / constraints
- [risk]
- [risk]
- [constraint]

## Next steps
- [next 30 days]
- [next 60–90 days]

## Leadership asks
1. [approval needed]
2. [decision needed]
3. [owner or resource needed]

## Bottom line
[one short paragraph summarizing the practical recommendation]
```

## Compression reminders

When the draft is too long, cut in this order:
1. historical background
2. technical detail
3. secondary options
4. explanatory prose around bullets

Never cut:
- the actual recommendation
- the decision required
- the near-term next steps
- major risks that could change the recommendation
